Technology Partnerships

Build with Multikernel

We work with cloud providers, hardware vendors, AI platform teams, and infrastructure software vendors to validate Multikernel on their platforms and integrate it with their products. The code is open source, and the results are measured on real hardware.

Discuss a Partnership Explore the Technology

Who We Work With

Teams that build the platforms Linux and AI workloads run on. If you ship an appliance with an embedded operating system, see Multikernel for OEMs.

Cloud and GPU Providers

Multikernel runs on bare-metal servers and on the VMs your customers already rent. We validate it on your instance types so that customers can give each workload its own kernel, with dedicated cores and native hardware access. Multikernel Private Cloud

Hardware Vendors

We bring up Multikernel on your servers, processors, and accelerators, add your platform to our validation matrix, and contribute the enablement work to upstream Linux. See Multikernel on ARM and RISC-V.

AI Platform Builders

Run agents and generated code in sandboxes with kernel-level isolation, on infrastructure you control. Design partners integrate Multikernel Sandbox into their orchestration pipeline with our engineers working alongside theirs. Design partner program

Infrastructure Software Vendors

Make your orchestration, observability, security, or developer tooling work with hosts where every workload runs on its own kernel. We build and test the integration with your engineers.

How a Partnership Works

There is no fixed program. Each partnership starts from one concrete technical goal and goes as far as the results justify.

01

Validate

Run Multikernel on your platform or with your workload. We test compatibility and performance on the real system and document the configuration that works.

02

Integrate

Fit Multikernel into your product, your deployment tooling, or your AI orchestration workflow, with our engineers working alongside your team.

03

Publish

Share the outcome as a validated configuration, a technical guide, or a joint demonstration, so that your customers can deploy what we built.

What to Expect

The same terms apply to every partnership, whatever its size.

Open Source Base

Multikernel is built on upstream Linux and licensed under GPL-2.0. You can evaluate and prototype before any agreement is in place, and joint engineering work is contributed upstream.

Measured Results

Compatibility and performance are measured on your hardware with your workloads, not projected from synthetic benchmarks. You receive the numbers and the configuration behind them.

Direct Engineering Access

You work with the engineers who build Multikernel, from the first conversation through integration.
