Volume and Multi-Year Terms

Volume discounts start at 100 nodes, and a multi-year prepaid term locks the price. Node counts combine across Private Cloud and LiveUpdate Standalone.

Fleets above 2,000 nodes are priced under a site agreement. Appliance vendors license per shipped controller: see Multikernel for OEMs.
